Review of **02-features.md only**; checkpoint 1 used as context.

- **BLOCKER — Bootstrap visibility and sufficiency (B2–4).** Depth-scaled steps and a maximum board-to-tool lever arm do not establish visibility in both modes. Eye-in-hand also needs camera-to-tool bounds and rotational field-of-view displacement. Derive conservative steps from actual image margins, depth and mount bounds. Increase excitation incrementally; require adequate information before enlarging the motion envelope. Replace the single estimated centring move with bounded, checked increments. Treat CAD guesses as uncertain.

- **BLOCKER — IPPE exemption undermines initialization (D3).** Remove the seed/probe exemption. Preserve both positive-depth hypotheses until robot-motion consistency resolves them, or acquire informative tilted views. Ambiguity arises under weak perspective, including distant tilted boards. Gate using noise-scaled error separation **and pose separation**: nearly identical solutions should not trigger rejection. Multi-frame agreement cannot establish the correct branch. [IPPE author’s explanation](https://github.com/tobycollins/IPPE#resolving-the-flip-ambiguity).

- **BLOCKER — Symmetry resolution is circular (E1–2).** The initial solve needs consistent corner indexing, while branch selection assumes a reliable initial solve. For v1, require orientation-identifiable targets—ChArUco, marked checkerboards, or explicitly tested parity-asymmetric boards—and defer general symmetric-board handling. Otherwise implement joint discrete branch selection with multiple initializations. A consistent global relabelling changes the nuisance board transform; mixed per-view relabellings violate the joint robot-constrained residual. ChArUco IDs resolve indexing ambiguity, while IPPE ambiguity still applies.

- **BLOCKER — “Accuracy gates” overclaim (E7/F).** Standard errors and split-half agreement measure conditional precision/stability; shared intrinsic, robot-kinematic and board errors can bias every estimate. Rename these gates accordingly. Specify a full 12-parameter tangent-space covariance, noise assumptions and nuisance-transform correlations. SciPy returns a **modified** Jacobian under robust loss; its inverse normal matrix alone does not establish sampling covariance. Use a justified robust covariance estimator or capture-level bootstrap. [SciPy result definition](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.optimize.least_squares.html).

- **SHOULD-FIX — Make the geometry explicit (A/E).** The unified model is valid. With \(G_i={}^{base}T_{tool}\), \(C_i={}^{camera}T_{board}\), \(X\) published and \(Z\) the other unknown, choose \(A_i=G_i^{-1}\) for eye-to-hand, \(A_i=G_i\) for eye-in-hand, and \(B_i=C_i^{-1}\). Then predict \( \hat C_i=X^{-1}A_i^{-1}Z\). Refine these two transforms with fixed intrinsics. Specify OpenCV input/output inversions; recover \(Z\) for `calibrateHandEye` seeds before scoring robot-predicted reprojection. Thus solver-input normalization also belongs in the mode adapter. [OpenCV conventions](https://docs.opencv.org/4.10.0/d9/d0c/group__calib3d.html).

- **SHOULD-FIX — Consensus loses the measurement model (D2/D6).** Averaged corners paired with one tool pose need not correspond to any physical exposure. Retain each frame’s corners, IDs, timestamp and associated TF; alternatively specify and validate a stationary-pose aggregation approximation. Align symmetry/pose hypotheses before comparing frames, and avoid treating correlated frames as independent precision gains.

- **SHOULD-FIX — Visibility needs geometric details (C1–2).** Define the board-centre pivot and deprojection of sampled \((u,v)\) under the selected camera model. Require positive depth, the printed face toward the camera, and sufficient projected marker-module width. Sample distorted outline edges where necessary; four projected vertices alone need not bound them. Include preliminary-estimate uncertainty in visibility margins.

- **SHOULD-FIX — Diversity and ordering need different criteria (C6–8/E5–6).** Farthest-point pose spacing does not guarantee informative motions. Use **relative** rotation axes and amplitudes, then check the selected set’s scaled Jacobian rank and smallest singular values. A raw condition number depends on translation/rotation scaling. Order retained IK solutions by joint travel and revalidate from the actual predecessor; Cartesian proximity does not establish short wrist travel.

- **SHOULD-FIX — Specify robust rejection precisely (E3–4).** Define noise scaling, `f_scale`, capture residual aggregation and a MAD floor. Compute rejection residuals from the joint robot-predicted projections. If removal leaves insufficient or degenerate data, fail; rerun observability after rejection. Reuse [`scipy.stats.median_abs_deviation`](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.stats.median_abs_deviation.html).

- **SHOULD-FIX — Strengthen capture gates and cut marker-depth masking (D3/D5).** Require spatially spread, noncollinear ChArUco corners and image sharpness; corner count alone is insufficient. Refine IPPE candidates before applying tight reprojection gates using existing [OpenCV pose refinement](https://docs.opencv.org/4.10.0/d5/d1f/calib3d_solvePnP.html). Defer custom per-marker depth masking: those poses inherit planar ambiguity and assumed scale. Reuse [existing board-consistency checks](https://github.com/opencv/opencv/blob/4.10.0/modules/objdetect/src/aruco/charuco_detector.cpp); marker removal must also recompute affected ChArUco corners.

- **SHOULD-FIX — Define leakage-free verification (E8/G1).** Refit both unknowns and perform training-data filtering independently inside every fold. Score held-out corners using predicted geometry without fitting held-out board poses. Group frames by capture. Require observable halves/folds and report failed splits; compare translations and SO(3) angular differences.

- **SHOULD-FIX — Bound simulation claims (H).** A homography renderer honestly validates planar pinhole geometry and synthetic integration. `warpPerspective` alone cannot validate nonzero lens distortion; add a distortion remap. Use an independently implemented geometry oracle, separate true poses from noisy reported robot poses, and add expected-failure cases: parallel axes, branch flips, bad timestamps and systematic model errors. Supplement simulation tuning with real-image fixtures; require failures as well as successful recovery.

- **SHOULD-FIX — Defer exposure search from v1 (I).** Clean detection is not guaranteed to form one monotonic interval, so generic bracketing/bisection is unjustified. If retained, use bounded coarse logarithmic sampling before local refinement; disable competing automatic controls, respect driver ranges/quantization, wait for settings to reach acquired frames, provide an ROI fallback when detection fails, cap exposure for blur, and restore settings on exit.

- **NIT — Reuse and wording (D4/K).** SB already returns subpixel corners; additional `cornerSubPix` needs evidence of benefit. Original-image refinement does not guarantee CLAHE introduces zero localization bias. Distinguish square counts from inner-corner counts, and use OpenCV’s calibration-pattern generator for ordinary checkerboards rather than assuming `Board.generateImage` covers them. [OpenCV detector and pattern documentation](https://docs.opencv.org/4.10.0/d9/d0c/group__calib3d.html).
